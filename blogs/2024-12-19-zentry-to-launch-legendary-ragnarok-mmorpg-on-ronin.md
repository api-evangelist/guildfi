Ronin and Zentry have formed a strategic partnership to bring the iconic Ragnarok MMORPG to the Ronin blockchain, with the ambitious goal of onboarding 50 million global users into Web3 and advancing mainstream adoption. Leveraging the success of Ragnarok Landverse , where the game has already demonstrated the potential of Web3 infrastructure, Zentry is now preparing to publish the title on Ronin. As one of the fastest-growing gaming blockchains, Ronin provides the ideal platform for Ragnarok Landverse with its thriving ecosystem featuring popular Web3 games like Axie Infinity and Pixels.
