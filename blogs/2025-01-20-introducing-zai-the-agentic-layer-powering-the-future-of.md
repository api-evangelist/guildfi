zAI is Zentry’s agentic layer — where AI agents live, work, and collaborate — aimed at transforming your everyday life into an MMORPG adventure. It focuses on creating a new breed of AI agents to tackle roles requiring intelligence, engagement, and open collaboration beyond what human- or protocol-based systems alone can achieve, while integrating external AI agent networks for coordination and data exchange. By changing how we interact with AI technology, zAI redefines entertainment primitives and catalyzes the rising human–agentic economy.
